# Code of Conduct

IR Lab is currently maintained by one person, with the possibility of other
contributors joining over time. This document sets expectations now so they're
in place before that happens, not after.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and any other project
  discussion.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or behavior.

## Reporting

If something violates this, or you experience/witness unacceptable behavior related
to this project, contact the maintainer at omar.bounawara.py@gmail.com. Reports will
be handled privately and taken seriously.

## Enforcement

As a small project, enforcement is currently at the maintainer's discretion --
ranging from a private conversation to removal of comments/contributions to a ban
from the project, depending on severity. This will be formalized further if and
when the contributor base grows.
